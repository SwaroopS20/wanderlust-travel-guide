Featured Destinations

Handpicked Wonderful locations for you.

Mumbai

Mumbai is the financial capital of India, famous for its colonial architecture, beaches, street food, Bollywood, and vibrant nightlife.

Delhi

Delhi is India's capital city, known for its rich history, Mughal monuments, bustling markets, and delicious street food.

Goa

Goa is India's beach paradise, famous for its sandy beaches, Portuguese heritage, churches, seafood, and nightlife.

Jaipur

Jaipur, the Pink City, is known for its majestic forts, palaces, vibrant bazaars, and rich Rajasthani culture.

Agra

Agra is home to the iconic Taj Mahal, a UNESCO World Heritage site, and is known for its Mughal architecture and rich history.

Udaipur

Udaipur, the City of Lakes, is known for its stunning palaces, lakes, and rich cultural heritage.

Darjeeling

Darjeeling, nestled in the Himalayas, is famous for its tea plantations, panoramic views of the mountains, and the Darjeeling Himalayan Railway.

Shimla

Shimla, the capital of Himachal Pradesh, is a hill station known for its colonial architecture, scenic beauty, and pleasant climate.

Rishikesh

Rishikesh, known as the Yoga Capital of the World, is a spiritual destination and a hub for adventure sports on the banks of the Ganges.
